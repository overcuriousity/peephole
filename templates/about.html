About

When you connect anything to the internet, you get scanned. We claim the right to scan back.

Every request against our configured interfaces is heuristically evaluated and classified; in consequence, appropriate reconnaissance measures are taken. The generated reconnaissance data is then used to create research datasets and threat intelligence.

Node operators are advised to configure their peephole instances in such a way that they do not apply to legitimate web requests on their official sites: normal visitors are usually not subject to this project.

We process IP addresses and the requests sent from them on the basis of our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: protecting our and others' systems against attacks, and researching the scanning that precedes them. Request contents are never published; public pages show addresses and aggregates only, delayed.

The source code is public: {{ crate::admin::views::REPO }}. The threat intelligence is available through the public API.
