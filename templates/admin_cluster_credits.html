How credits work

A fixed pool a day for the members anyone can reach, earned by selling goods, spent on goods. Every figure is this node's own count, from its copy of the log.

Every day 1000 credits are split evenly among the members anyone can reach: advertised listeners that at least half of the members reached in at least 12 of the day's 24 hours. Nothing else creates credits and nothing destroys them, but what a member that does not earn here still holds of its pool shares; a credit is gone 7 days after the day of its pool. Everything else is earned by selling: lookups, names, probes, scans, audits and relays.

Each price follows sales: it rises while the good sells (or every slot is taken) and falls while it does not, down to nothing. What this node answers itself is free, though its own scan jobs are paid from its balance. The price rises while demand is above supply.

L1 to L5: what one delivered result costs per level, the price divided by the scanner's success rate there against the best live scanner. A failed scan is not paid, so jobs go to the cheapest per delivered result. {{ price.weights_as_of }}

A credit can be used on the day it was earned and the 6 days after, whoever holds it by then.

Every node keeps what it earns. A node whose lookup needs more than it holds draws the rest from the others, the richest first.

What is charged goes to the node that answered. An offer without an answer lapses after 15 minutes (a scan offer after the longest scan) and costs nothing.
