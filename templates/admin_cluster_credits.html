Where credits come from

Every day 1000 credits are split evenly among the members anyone can reach: advertised listeners that at least half of the members reached in at least 12 of the day's 24 hours. Nothing else creates credits and nothing destroys them, but what a member that does not earn here still holds of its pool shares; a credit is gone 7 days after the day of its pool. Everything else is earned by selling: lookups, names, probes, scans, audits and relays.
